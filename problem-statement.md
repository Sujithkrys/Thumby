# Problem Statement — Thumby

## Who is this for

Anyone with a YouTube channel or Instagram page who needs thumbnails. Kept broad since the core mechanism (browse proven prompts, generate) delivers the same value regardless of niche. Launch categories: gaming, tech, vlogs, beauty, finance.

## What is the pain today

1. Outsourcing to freelancers/editors is expensive, and cost scales with volume.
2. Most creators lack design skills, so they pay professionals.
3. AI thumbnail tools exist, but people don't know about them or struggle to get good results from them.
4. Getting a good AI result takes iteration and prompt skill most people don't have.
5. Coming up with an eye-catching concept in the first place is its own struggle, separate from execution.

## What this platform does about it

1. User provides a prompt directly — optionally selecting a category, an existing gallery thumbnail, or their own uploaded image as a generation reference — and the platform generates from that input.
2. Delivers professional-quality results at a fraction of freelancer/designer cost, quickly.
3. *Should-have:* explains the reasoning behind design choices, teaching users over time.
4. *Future (explicitly labeled):* MCP integrations for connecting to other LLMs/tools.

## Success metrics

1. Primary: user returns and generates again within 7 days of first use.
2. Secondary: converts to paid within 30 days (placeholder — adjust once real data exists).

## Explicitly out of scope for this version

1. Editing the output image — stickers, manual text, handwriting.
2. Upscaling.
3. Filters.
